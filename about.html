Who We Are

Omtech Corporation is an industrial consultancy, corporate capability-building, and engineering solutions platform founded by Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra, drawing on over 39+ years of industrial experience across premier organisations including Kuwait Oil Company (KOC), NTPC, and IPCL.

We help industrial enterprises develop capable engineers, responsible leaders, and safer workplaces. Our multidisciplinary expertise spans Technical & Reliability Training, Leadership Development, Safety & Operational Discipline, Management System Audits, Cybersecurity, and Turnkey Fire Protection & Life Safety.

By combining deep shop-floor diagnostic experience with adult-learning pedagogy and engineering rigor, we deliver actionable solutions that enhance plant reliability, improve Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF), eliminate recurring breakdowns, and embed an uncompromising safety and compliance culture.

Execution Methodology

How We Manage Assignments

A disciplined 6-stage delivery framework ensuring measurable plant-floor impact and sustainable competency growth:

Step 01

Training-Needs Discussion

Direct technical consultation with plant leadership and HR to identify operational challenges, repeat equipment failures, and workforce capability gaps.

Step 02

Participant & Plant Context Review

Review of cohort experience levels, plant P&ID schematics, maintenance history, and operating envelopes to contextualize learning modules.

Step 03

Customised Proposal & Syllabus

Preparation of a transparent proposal detailing specific learning outcomes, workshop schedule, simulation exercises, faculty credentials, and commercial terms.

Step 04

Programme Delivery & Simulations

Interactive on-site or virtual delivery combining solid engineering theory with genuine plant failure case studies, schematic drills, and team problem-solving.

Step 05

Assessment & Action Planning

Objective post-training competency evaluation and creation of practical shop-floor action plans that participants can immediately execute at their work stations.

Step 06

Agreed Workplace Follow-Up

Structured follow-up reviews where included in the agreed proposal, evaluating implementation progress, coaching engineers, and reinforcing operational discipline.

📦 Client Deliverables Included With Every Assignment

Comprehensive printed/digital participant workbooks, plant-specific failure case portfolios, individual competency assessment summaries, and ready-to-use shop-floor implementation action-plan templates.
