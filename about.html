Execution Methodology

Our Approach

We follow a structured and compliant project execution methodology:

Step 01

Site Survey & Risk Assessment

Comprehensive on-site audit of hazards, egress pathways, structural blueprints, and asset vulnerabilities.

Step 02

System Design & Engineering

Hydraulic calculations, loop layouts, CAD drafting, and equipment selection strictly complying with IS & NFPA codes.

Step 03

Supply & Installation

Procurement of certified components and meticulous installation by trained, certified technicians.

Step 04

Testing & Commissioning

Rigorous pressure testing, detector calibration, simulated alarm drills, and authority compliance sign-offs.

Step 05

Training & Documentation

Hands-on user training for security teams, as-built drawings handover, and emergency protocol documentation.

Step 06

Annual Maintenance & Support

Preventive AMC visits, 24/7 breakdown response, statutory audit renewals, and genuine spare parts supply.

🛡️ Every project is executed with a focus on quality, safety, and long-term reliability.
