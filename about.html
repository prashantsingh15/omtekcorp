About Omtek Corporation & Leadership

Built upon 39 years of real-world plant engineering, international energy operations, and proven corporate training expertise.

Kuwait Oil Company (KOC) Mechanical Maintenance Engineer (Emp #45338)
Founder & Principal Consultant

Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra

Omtek Corporation was founded under the vision of Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra, a renowned industrial leader and plant engineering authority with more than 39 years of hands-on industrial and international experience across the Middle East and India.

Throughout his distinguished career, including his service as a Mechanical Maintenance Engineer with Kuwait Oil Company (KOC), Mr. Mishra has spearheaded complex refinery turnarounds, process safety management (PSM) rollouts, reliability-centred maintenance (RCM) regimes, and critical asset integrity audits.

Unlike generic academic management firms, Mr. Mishra’s consulting and training philosophy is anchored strictly in shop-floor reality, failure analysis, and plant-level execution.

“Real industrial excellence is not achieved through theoretical binders on an executive shelf. It is achieved when engineers on the plant floor know how to trace a failure to its root cause, enforce zero-compromise safety, and optimize machinery to operate without unpredicted trips.”
