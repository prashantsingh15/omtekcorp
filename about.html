About Omtek Corporation & Leadership

Built upon 39 years of real-world plant engineering, international energy operations, and proven corporate training expertise.

Verified Professional Career Experience Former Engineer: KOC, NTPC & IPCL
Founder & Principal Consultant

Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra

Omtek Corporation was founded under the vision of Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra, an industrial leader and engineering specialist with over 39 years of founder career experience across major energy, petrochemical, and power generation enterprises.

His career includes prior engineering service with premier organizations such as Kuwait Oil Company (KOC), NTPC, and IPCL. Throughout his 39-year career, Mr. Mishra has gained practical expertise in mechanical maintenance, rotating equipment reliability, plant turnarounds, and process safety advisory.

Our consulting and training philosophy is anchored in practical, plant-oriented learning — using real shop-floor failure logs, P&ID engineering drawings, and hands-on operational exercises to build lasting plant competence.

“Operational excellence is built on practical engineering principles: identifying root causes, executing structured maintenance, and fostering a zero-compromise safety culture across every shift.”
