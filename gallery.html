Industrial Case Studies & Field Troubleshooting

Selected engineering case studies illustrating root cause diagnostics, evidence analysis, corrective action, and operational outcomes.

Industrial Engineering Case Study

Centrifugal Pump Mechanical Seal Failure Analysis

Problem: Hydrocarbon booster pump experiencing recurrent mechanical seal leakage every 45 days, causing unplanned unit slowdowns.

Evidence Reviewed: API Plan 32 flush flow rates, seal face thermography logs, vibration FFT spectrums, and suction strainer differential pressure records.

Corrective Action: Re-engineered flush piping geometry to eliminate vapor locking, retrofitted API Plan 53B dual pressurized seal barrier, and recalibrated laser alignment to < 0.02 mm.

Outcome: Zero seal leaks recorded over 24 months of continuous operation; improved MTBF and reduced MTTR significantly.

Industrial Engineering Case Study

High-Pressure Boiler Feed Pump Axial Vibration

Problem: Severe axial vibration tripping 1.2 MW high-pressure boiler feed pump during boiler load transitions.

Evidence Reviewed: Thrust bearing RTD temperature trends, balance disc wear clearance measurements, and dynamic rotor response curves.

Corrective Action: Re-machined balance disc mating faces, re-established design thrust clearances, and modified boiler warmup ramp rates.

Outcome: Reduced axial vibration by 76% to within ISO 10816 Class IV acceptable limits, restoring full plant steam reliability.

Industrial Safety Case Study

Process Safety Management (PSM) Gap Closure

Problem: Petrochemical facility needed comprehensive audit and gap remediation for OSHA 1910.119 PSM compliance.

Evidence Reviewed: Process Safety Information (PSI), P&ID drawings, Management of Change (MOC) logs, and LEL detector placement maps.

Corrective Action: Conducted 14-element PSM audit, updated Permit-to-Work (PTW) LOTO procedures, and relocated gas detectors to high-risk leak nodes.

Outcome: Successfully passed statutory safety audit with zero non-conformities; established institutionalized safety culture.
