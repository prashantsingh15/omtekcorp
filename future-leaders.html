THE INDUSTRIAL CHALLENGE

The Transition From Technical Expert to People Manager

In continuous-process plants and heavy engineering industries, the greatest technical specialists are frequently promoted into supervisory roles without receiving structured management or interpersonal training. The resulting gap often creates operational friction, micromanagement, poor cross-functional coordination, and high attrition.

Omtech Corporation’s Future Leaders Development curriculum addresses this exact workplace need. Through industrial role-plays, simulated shift handovers, group decision exercises, and customized workplace action plans, we teach technical minds how to delegate, communicate with non-technical stakeholders, coach subordinates, and take complete ownership of plant safety and output.

Practical Role-Plays Simulated shift conflicts, breakdown handovers, and performance feedback.
Ownership Culture Moving from "blame-passing" to structured operational accountability.
Action Plan Portfolio Every participant leaves with an immediate 90-day workplace action plan.
Participant Outcome: Participants develop emotional intelligence, structured communication, and decisive problem-solving skills rooted in high-hazard operational reality.
CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE

8 Core Leadership Development Programmes

Standardized outlines detailing intended participants, workplace problems addressed, practical activities, and takeaways:

FL-01 | LEADERSHIP TRANSITION ⏱ 2 DAYS / 16 HRS

Technical Expert to People Manager

Workplace Problem: Top technical performers struggling to delegate, feeling overwhelmed by administrative duties, and managing through command-and-control rather than influence.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Shifting mindset from "doing" to "enabling others"
• Situational leadership models for plant floor teams
• Handling former peers with authority and empathy
• Managing shift handovers and daily operational meetings
Practical Activities: Shift handover simulation, difficult peer feedback role-play, and personalized time-reallocation matrix.
Details
FL-02 / OTC-23 | ACCOUNTABILITY ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Ownership, Accountability & Plant Ethics

Workplace Problem: Chronic inter-departmental finger-pointing between operations, maintenance, and safety when equipment trips or deadlines slip.

Key Learning Outcomes: • The Ladder of Accountability (Victim vs. Accountable stance)
• Fostering psychological safety without compromising standards
• Ethical compliance in logs, permits, and reporting
• Single-point operational responsibility across shift boundaries
Practical Activities: Breakdown incident post-mortem drill, "No Excuse" accountability contracts, and case review of near-miss reporting ethics.
Syllabus 📄
FL-03 | COMMUNICATION ⏱ 1 TO 2 DAYS

Communication & Cross-Functional Coordination

Workplace Problem: Jargon-heavy reporting, poor coordination during turnaround maintenance, and lack of alignment between field engineers and commercial/finance teams.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Translating technical risks into business impact for executive review
• Effective daily tool-box talks (TBT) and safety briefings
• Managing conflict between Production targets and Maintenance windows
• Precise logbook and email communication standards
Practical Activities: 3-minute executive elevator pitch drill, contentious turnaround coordination simulation, and daily TBT delivery assessment.
Details
FL-04 | PEOPLE COACHING ⏱ 2 DAYS / 16 HRS

Delegation, Coaching & Team Development

Workplace Problem: Supervisors working 14-hour days doing manual technician tasks because "it's faster to do it myself than explain it."

Key Learning Outcomes: • The 5 Levels of Delegation and authority boundary definition
• The GROW coaching model for frontline technical troubleshooting
• Skill vs. Will matrix for assigning plant maintenance tasks
• Providing constructive feedback without demotivating workers
Practical Activities: Live coaching role-play (coaching an errant technician on pump seal alignment), and task delegation mapping canvas.
Details
FL-05 | DECISION-MAKING ⏱ 2 DAYS / 16 HRS

Problem-Solving & Operational Decision-Making

Workplace Problem: Analysis paralysis during emergency plant trips, treating superficial symptoms rather than root causes, and repeat mistakes.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Fast-track 5-Why and Fishbone diagnostics during shift upsets
• Deciding under operational pressure: Shutdown vs. De-rate vs. Online repair
• Risk assessment matrices (Likelihood vs. Severity) in real-time
• Capturing lessons learned and updating Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
Practical Activities: High-pressure simulated gas leak and pump cavitation scenario where participants must make decisions under tight time limits.
Details
FL-06 | SAFETY LEADERSHIP ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Safety Leadership & Operational Discipline for Supervisors

Workplace Problem: Safety viewed as an administrative police function rather than a core line-management responsibility.

Key Learning Outcomes: • The Supervisor's legal and moral safety mandate
• Conducting non-punitive safety walk-throughs and interventions
• Exercising Stop-Work Authority without fear of production retribution
• Contractor safety supervision and Permit-to-Work compliance
Practical Activities: Video hazard spotting audit, challenging an unsafe contractor manager role-play, and stop-work protocol simulation.

FL-07 | MENTORING FRAMEWORK ⏱ 2 DAYS + COACHING

Young Engineer Mentoring & Onboarding

Workplace Problem: Fresh graduate engineers feeling lost, taking up to 18 months to become productive, and high GET attrition rates.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Setting up a structured 90-day Buddy and Mentoring architecture
• Fast-tracking plant blueprint fluency and equipment familiarization
• Career pathing and professional work ethics development
• Regular mentoring check-in templates and milestone reviews
Practical Activities: Designing a tailored 90-day GET competency roadmap, mentor-mentee pairing simulation, and progress review mock sessions.
Details
FL-08 / OTC-24 | INTERNAL FACULTY ⏱ 2 DAYS / 16 HRS

Technical Train-the-Trainer

Workplace Problem: Seasoned plant engineers have deep knowledge but lack adult pedagogy skills to structure workshops and engage learners.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Principles of adult learning (Andragogy) for technical audiences
• Designing modular training slides, schematics, and interactive drills
• Managing difficult or passive participants in classroom settings
• Objective competency assessment and question design
Practical Activities: 15-minute live micro-teaching session delivered by each participant, with video recording and peer/faculty critique.
