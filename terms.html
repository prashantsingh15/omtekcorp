Terms of Advisory Service & Engagement

Governance guidelines for industrial consulting, HAZOP studies, and corporate safety training engagements.

1. Professional Engagement & Scope of Work

Omtek Corporation delivers high-level industrial advisory, process safety audits (PSM, HAZOP, HAZID), and corporate training programs. All consulting deliverables are defined under formal Statements of Work (SOW) executed between Omtek Corporation and the client organization.

2. Regulatory Standards Compliance

Our recommendations, safety training modules, and engineering audit frameworks comply with recognized national and international safety mandates including OSHA 1910.119, OISD standards, NFPA guidelines, and ASME technical codes.

3. Intellectual Property Rights

All training curriculum slides, engineering methodologies, calculation templates, and workshop materials provided by Omtek Corporation remain the intellectual property of Omtek Corporation, licensed to the client organization for internal capability building.

4. Corporate Inquiries & Invoicing

Official invoices and payment terms for corporate engagements are issued upon milestone completion as specified in the agreed-upon agreement. For queries, please contact omtechcorp2402@gmail.com.
