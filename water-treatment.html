Turnkey Water, Wastewater & Environmental Solutions

Modern process industries face stringent discharge limits imposed by Central Pollution Control Boards (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (SPCB), alongside mounting raw water costs. Omtek Corporation delivers high-level chemical engineering advisory, plant optimization, membrane life extension, and turnkey engineering.

1. Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) & Membrane Systems

High-recovery Reverse Osmosis systems designed for brackish water (BWRO) and seawater desalination (SWRO). We optimize recovery rates up to 85% while minimizing bio-fouling and mineral scaling.

  • Pre-treatment design: MMF, ACF, Softeners & Ultrafiltration (UF)
  • Antiscalant & De-chlorination chemical dosing optimization
  • RO Membrane Autopsy & Clean-In-Place (CIP) cleaning protocols
  • High-pressure pump energy recovery turbine integration

2. Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) for Chemical Effluents

Tailored physio-chemical and biological treatment systems for high BOD, COD, heavy metal, and toxic chemical streams from Refineries, Petrochemicals, Textiles, and Electroplating units.

  • Primary Treatment: Coagulation, Flocculation, DAF & Neutralization
  • Secondary Biological: ASP, MBBR (Moving Bed) & SBR Technology
  • Heavy metal precipitation (Chrome, Lead, Zinc) & Color removal
  • Sludge dewatering: High-pressure Filter Press & Decanter Centrifuges

3. Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) & Advanced MBR

Compact, odor-free STP solutions utilizing Membrane Bio-Reactor (MBR) and Sequential Batch Reactor (SBR) technology for industrial townships, commercial complexes, and municipal plants.

  • Submerged MBR ultrafiltration hollow-fiber membrane systems
  • Nitrification & Denitrification for Nitrogen & Phosphorus removal
  • Ozonation, UV Disinfection & Chlorine Dioxide dosing
  • Treated water recycling for flush reuse, cooling tower & greenbelt

4. Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) Thermal Evaporation

Complete ZLD system design integrating High Recovery RO, Multi-Effect Evaporators (MEE), Mechanical Vapor Recompression (MVR), and Agitated Thin Film Dryers (ATFD).

  • Thermal MVR & MEE steam efficiency optimization
  • Crystallizer & ATFD solid salt cake recovery (NaCl, Na2SO4)
  • Condensate Polishing Units (CPU) for 100% loop recycling
  • Zero liquid discharge compliance certification for regulatory audit

5. Demineralization (DM), EDI & High-Purity Boiler Water

Ultra-pure water generation for high-pressure power generation boilers, pharmaceutical formulations, and microelectronics manufacturing using Cation-Anion-Mixed Bed Ion Exchange and EDI.

  • Two-bed SAC-SBA & Mixed Bed Ion Exchange resin regeneration
  • Continuous Electrodeionization (EDI) chemical-free polishing
  • Conductivity control (< 0.1 µS/cm) & Silica reduction (< 5 ppb)
  • Resin capacity health evaluation & fouling diagnostics

6. Cooling Water & Boiler Water Dosing Chemistry

Comprehensive chemical treatment programs to prevent corrosion, scale formation, bio-fouling, and microbiological growth in industrial cooling towers, condensers, and steam boilers.

  • Scale & corrosion inhibitors (Phosphonates, Polymeric Dispersants)
  • Biocide treatment against Legionella & SRB (Sulfate Reducing Bacteria)
  • Boiler internal treatment: Oxygen scavengers & Volatile Amines
  • Blowdown water recovery & Cycle of Concentration (COC) maximization

Frequently Asked Questions (Water & ZLD Solutions)

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) is an advanced water treatment process in which all industrial effluent is purified and recycled, leaving zero liquid discharge into public water bodies or land. CPCB mandates ZLD for red-category high-polluting industries (Textiles, Refineries, Distilleries, Pharmaceuticals) to prevent groundwater contamination and conserve freshwater reserves.
RO membrane premature failure is primarily caused by improper antiscalant dosing, biofouling, or abrasive silt carryover. Omtek conducts thorough Silt Density Index (SDI) analysis, optimizes pre-treatment filtration (UF/ACF), prescribes customized CIP chemical regimes, and monitors normalized differential pressure to extend membrane life beyond 5 years.
Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) uses floating plastic media to grow bio-film culture and relies on secondary clarifiers for solids separation. Membrane Bio-Reactor (MBR) integrates ultrafiltration membranes directly into the biological tank, eliminating secondary clarifiers and producing crystal-clear effluent suitable for direct RO feed and toilet flushing.
