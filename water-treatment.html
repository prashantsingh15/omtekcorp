Systematic Project Execution

Plumbing Design, Installation & Commissioning

We follow a systematic project execution approach to ensure quality, structural durability, and absolute reliability. Every project undergoes rigorous testing before handover.

🔧 Bathroom Rough-In & Fixture Fitting Assembly
🧪 Pipe Spool Fabrication & Hydrostatic Test Yard
💻 Automated Pressure Testing & Digital Diagnostics
STAGE 01

Site Survey & Requirement Analysis

Comprehensive on-site topography inspection, fixture count, peak-demand calculations, and municipal source evaluation.

STAGE 02

System Design & Hydraulic Calculations

Engineering sizing of piping headers, frictional head-loss calculations, pump NPSH checks, and ISO/IS plumbing code compliance.

STAGE 03

Material Selection & Procurement

Procurement of certified GI, CPVC, UPVC, HDPE, and PPR pipes with batch mill test certificates and high-grade valving.

STAGE 04

Installation by Skilled Technicians

Precision jointing, solvent welding, mechanical threading, robust pipe clamping, acoustic insulation, and seismic bracing.

STAGE 05

Pressure Testing & Leak Detection

Hydrostatic pressure holding tests at 1.5x working pressure for 24 hours, ultrasonic leak checks, and flow rate validation.

STAGE 06

Commissioning & Handover

Line disinfection & chlorination flush, automated booster calibration, As-Built documentation handover, and operator training.
