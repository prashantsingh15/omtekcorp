Industries We Serve

Deep engineering domain authority across heavy manufacturing, energy utilities, and critical infrastructure.

Tailored Sector Specializations

Critical Asset Sectors

Our advisory frameworks and training curriculums are customized to match the unique mechanical, electrical, and process safety challenges of each industry.

🛢️ Oil & Gas (Upstream & Downstream)

Specialized in wellhead safety, rotating machinery maintenance, gas gathering stations, and high-pressure manifold reliability adhering to API and OISD codes.

  • ✓ Hydrocarbon leak prevention & barrier management
  • ✓ Offshore & onshore fire deluge valve audits
  • ✓ Mechanical seal & centrifugal compressor reliability

🏭 Petroleum Refineries

Supporting CDUs, VDUs, FCCUs, and Hydrocrackers during major planned turnarounds to compress schedules, ensure zero safety incidents, and verify static equipment integrity.

  • ✓ Turnaround & Shutdown Management (TAM) execution
  • ✓ Risk-Based Inspection (RBI) & RCM implementation
  • ✓ High-temperature hydrogen attack (HTHA) surveillance

🧪 Petrochemicals & Polymer Plants

Preventing runaway reactions and major toxic releases through comprehensive Process Safety Management (PSM), layer of protection analysis (LOPA), and SIL validation.

  • ✓ Flammable monomer handling safety
  • ✓ Toxic gas detection (LEL/PPM) cause & effect testing
  • ✓ Six Sigma defect reduction in polymer extrusion

⚡ Power Plants (Thermal & Combined Cycle)

Minimizing unplanned boiler trips, turbine bearing failures, and cooling tower water degradation through predictive maintenance and Root Cause Analysis (RCA).

  • ✓ Boiler tube failure RCA & mitigation
  • ✓ Turbine vibration analysis & condition monitoring
  • ✓ High-pressure steam piping PTW protocols

🏗️ EPC Contractors & Infrastructure

Assisting prime contractors during site erection, pre-commissioning walk-throughs, punch-list clearance, and client handover verification.

  • ✓ Fire protection piping hydrotest & flushing
  • ✓ Commissioning permit-to-work administration
  • ✓ Quality assurance & vendor document compliance

💧 Water & Wastewater Utilities

Consulting for heavy industrial RO facilities, zero liquid discharge (ZLD) crystallization systems, ETP/STP compliance, and chemical dosing optimization.

  • ✓ Membrane fouling diagnostics & cleaning cycles
  • ✓ Multi-effect evaporator (MEE) thermal optimization
  • ✓ Effluent statutory parameter conformance

🔩 Steel, Metals & Heavy Manufacturing

Optimizing continuous casting, rolling mills, and overhead cranes through predictive maintenance, Poka-Yoke error-proofing, and scrap reduction.

  • ✓ Hydraulic system contamination control
  • ✓ Poka-Yoke & CAPA in machining lines
  • ✓ Molten metal handling safety audits

🧱 Cement Industries

Rotary kiln alignment, clinker cooler maintenance, baghouse filtration efficiency, and heavy gear drive reliability engineering.

  • ✓ Kiln tyre lubrication & mechanical clearance
  • ✓ Dust explosion prevention & fire suppression
  • ✓ Energy efficiency audits in raw mill grinding
