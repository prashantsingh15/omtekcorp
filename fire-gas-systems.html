Turnkey Fire & Gas Lifecycle Engineering

Operating high-pressure hydrocarbon, LPG, LNG, and chemical handling facilities requires fault-tolerant Fire & Gas (F&G) Systems. A failure to detect gas leaks or trigger deluge suppression instantly can lead to catastrophic loss of life and equipment.

Omtek Corporation provides complete end-to-end engineering, procurement support, pre-commissioning, live testing, and turnkey Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) strictly following NFPA (72, 15, 16) and OISD-116/117 guidelines.

F&G Systems Engineering Scope

  • Mechanical Erection & Fire Network Piping
  • Pre-Commissioning, Loop Checking & Live Commissioning
  • LEL Hydrocarbon & Toxic (H2S, CO) Gas Detectors
  • IR/UV Flame Detectors & Optical Smoke Detection
  • High-Velocity Water Deluge & Foam Spray Systems
  • Dedicated Fire Water Ring Main Hydraulics
  • Cause & Effect Matrix Validation & PLC Interlocks
  • Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
