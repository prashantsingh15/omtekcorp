HSE & Process Safety Management (PSM)

Life safety engineering, HAZOP/HAZID studies, Permit to Work (PTW) design, and OSHA 1910.119 compliance for high-hazard industrial facilities.

Life Safety & Risk Mitigation

Zero Loss of Containment & Institutional Safety Culture

In high-hazard chemical processing plants, petroleum tank farms, power stations, and manufacturing complexes, failure to strictly enforce Process Safety Management (PSM) can lead to catastrophic explosions, toxic gas release, or heavy regulatory penalties.

Omtek Corporation delivers comprehensive Process Safety Management (PSM) Audits and HSE System Architecture aligned strictly with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119 and OISD Standards. We help operating companies build zero-harm plant environments.

Safety Engineering & Auditing Capabilities

  • Process Safety Management (PSM 14 Elements) Audits
  • HAZOP (Hazard & Operability Study) Moderation
  • HAZID & Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA)
  • Behaviour-Based Safety (BBS) Leadership Programs
  • Permit to Work (PTW) System Design & Audits
  • Job Safety Analysis (JSA) & Risk Registers
  • On-Site Emergency Preparedness & Crisis Response
  • Hazardous Area Classification (IS/IEC 60079) Audits
