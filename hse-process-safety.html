Our Audit Scope & 4-Stage Methodology

Every audit engagement is executed through structured governance, ensuring zero disruption to live operations while systematically preparing your plant for formal external certification.

STAGE 01

Documentation & Baseline Review

Evaluation of Quality/Safety Manuals, SOPs, statutory registers, and compliance records against target ISO standards.

STAGE 02

Shop-Floor Verification

On-site physical audits: verifying real shop-floor work practices, instrument calibration, PPE compliance, and PTW execution.

STAGE 03

Gap Analysis & NC Scoring

Detailed root-cause analysis of non-conformances (Major, Minor, and Observations) with risk prioritization matrix.

STAGE 04

CAPA Roadmap & Readiness

Formulating practical Corrective & Preventive Action plans and preparing documentation dossiers for certification bodies.

Tangible Audit Deliverables

At the conclusion of an Omtech audit engagement, plant leadership receives practical, executive-ready documentation to close operational vulnerabilities immediately:

  • ✓ Comprehensive Audit Findings Report: Categorized by severity (Major/Minor Non-Conformances and Opportunities for Improvement).
  • ✓ Prioritized CAPA Roadmap: Action plans with assigned ownership, target timelines, and verification criteria.
  • ✓ SOP Optimization Matrix: Practical updates to plant procedures ensuring alignment with shop-floor reality and standard clauses.
  • ✓ Pre-Certification Dossier: Structured audit evidence folder for seamless presentation to external accredited certification bodies.
*Note: Omtech Corporation provides management system readiness, gap analysis, and internal auditing advisory. Management system certification is issued by an appropriately accredited certification body.

Process Safety Management (PSM) & Technical Safety

Specialized high-hazard mitigation, HAZOP facilitation, and life-safety frameworks strictly aligned with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119 and OISD standards.

Zero Loss of Containment & Institutional Safety Culture

In high-hazard chemical processing plants, petroleum refineries, fertilizer complexes, and bulk storage tank farms, failure to enforce rigorous Process Safety Management (PSM) can lead to catastrophic explosions, toxic releases, or severe operational shutdowns.

Omtech Corporation delivers comprehensive Process Safety Management (PSM) Audits and Technical Risk Governance. We evaluate the 14 elements of OSHA PSM to ensure that safety barriers remain robust across every operating phase.

Process Safety & Technical Study Capabilities

  • OSHA 1910.119 PSM (14 Elements) Compliance Audits
  • HAZOP (Hazard & Operability Study) Facilitation & Gap Analysis
  • HAZID & Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA) Studies
  • Behaviour-Based Safety (BBS) Workforce Cultural Programs
  • Permit to Work (PTW) & Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Auditing
  • Job Safety Analysis (JSA) & Operational Risk Registers
  • Hazardous Area Classification (IS/IEC 60079) Audits
  • On-Site Emergency Response Planning (ERP) & Mock Drills
