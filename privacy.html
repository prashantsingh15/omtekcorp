Privacy Policy & Data Security

Our commitment to client confidentiality, process data protection, and industrial privacy.

1. Client Information Privacy & Confidentiality

At Omtek Corporation, we respect the sensitive operational nature of heavy industrial consulting, refinery audits, and Process Safety Management (PSM). Any information submitted through our RFQ contact forms, including company names, email addresses, phone numbers, and operational scope details, is strictly treated as confidential executive data.

2. Non-Disclosure & Industrial Audit Security

All plant-floor data, engineering schematics, HAZOP report findings, and corporate training logs shared with Omtek Corporation during technical engagements are protected under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). We never share, sell, or license client information to third-party brokers or external marketing vendors.

3. Cookies & Analytical Visitor Tracking

We utilize standard Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Tag Manager scripts to analyze website traffic performance, mobile load speeds, and geographic lead volume. This information is aggregated anonymously to optimize user experience and mobile responsiveness.

4. Data Access & Inquiries

Clients may request the deletion or update of their stored contact information at any time by emailing our compliance team at omtechcorp2402@gmail.com or calling our hotline (+91-9810772284 / +91-9987079111).
