Privacy Policy & Data Security

Our commitment to client confidentiality, process data protection, and industrial privacy.

Operational Privacy & Data Governance Summary

Omtech Corporation ("Omtech", "we", "us") values client confidentiality in all industrial consulting, corporate training, fire protection engineering, and plant audits. This policy outlines how inquiry data is processed, stored, and protected.

1. Client Information & Process Data Confidentiality

During the course of technical discussions, proposals, training needs assessments, or engineering plant audits, clients may share proprietary operational details (e.g., P&IDs, equipment tags, failure logs, shift schedules). All technical data and confidential client records are treated under strict non-disclosure obligations and are never shared, sold, or distributed to third parties.

2. Web Enquiry Processing & Receiving Inboxes

When you submit an enquiry through our website forms or proposal request modals, your submitted contact information (Name, Work Email, Phone Number, Organization, and Selected Programme/Service) is transmitted directly to our authorized executive business mailboxes (safehands@omtechcorporation.com and omtechcorp2402@gmail.com). This transmission is used solely to generate official technical proposals, schedule discussions, and respond to your direct inquiry.

3. Central Storage & Restricted Access Controls

All digital enquiry logs and proposal records are stored centrally within password-protected, encrypted Hostinger databases and secured company servers. Access to these records is strictly restricted to vetted company leadership and designated administrative personnel via authenticated portals. No public access or client-side exposure of lead data is permitted.

4. Data Retention & Archival Arrangements

Inquiry records and proposal files are retained for the active duration of the commercial engagement, or up to 24 months for statutory audit trail and warranty correspondence, after which they are securely purged or archived. Clients and individuals retain the right to request immediate deletion of their contact information at any time.

5. Analytics & Website Performance Tracking

Our website employs minimal, privacy-respecting performance analytics to monitor uptime, page rendering speeds, and aggregated visitor flows. We do not use third-party advertising tracking networks, behavioral remarketing cookies, or cross-site data broker profiling.

6. Data Subject Requests & Privacy Officer Contact

To review, update, or request permanent deletion of your inquiry details, or for queries concerning our confidentiality covenants, please contact our Data Governance Officer:
