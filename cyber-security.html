Protecting Plant Floor Control Networks (DCS, PLC, SCADA)

As oil refineries, petrochemical complexes, power grids, and water utilities digitize their operations, the boundary between Enterprise IT networks and Operational Technology (OT) has converged. A cyber compromise on a refinery's Distributed Control System (DCS) or Emergency Shutdown (ESD) panel can lead to catastrophic plant trips, physical asset destruction, or hazardous releases.

Omtek Corporation delivers specialized Industrial Cybersecurity Advisory, OT Network Vulnerability Audits, and Purdue Model Segmentation aligned with IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82 standards, bridging physical process engineering with defense-in-depth cyber security.

Defense-In-Depth Architecture

Purdue Model OT Security Breakdown

We audit and isolate every layer of your industrial hierarchy to prevent lateral movement of malware from IT to plant control systems.

Level 0 & Level 1

Field Instrumentation & Controllers

Sensors, transmitters, actuators, PLCs, RTUs, and Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS). Hardening Modbus, Profibus, and Ethernet/IP protocol integrity.

Level 2 & Level 3

Area Control & Site Operations

DCS Operator Consoles, HMI Stations, Alarm Servers, Plant Historians, and Batch Engineering Workstations. Restricting USB media and patching legacy OS.

Level 3.5 (IDMZ)

Industrial DMZ Boundary

Dual-homed firewalls, Jump Hosts, OT Active Directory isolation, and Secure Remote Access (PAM) for OEM vendor maintenance.

IT Security vs. OT Security Matrix

Why standard IT security tools fail in industrial control plant environments:

Evaluation Parameter Enterprise IT Security Industrial Operational Technology (OT) Security
Primary Objective Data Confidentiality & Privacy Physical Safety, Process Availability & Human Life
System Downtime Tolerance Reboot / Maintenance Windows Accepted 0 Downtime (Continuous 24/7/365 Production)
Vulnerability Scanning Active Port Scanning & Penetration Testing Passive Network Monitoring Only (Active scanning can trip PLCs)
Protocol Architecture TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, TLS Encryption Modbus TCP, DNP3, PROFINET, OPC UA, BACnet (Unencrypted Legacy)
Patching Frequency Weekly / Monthly Automatic Updates Strict Change Management (Quarterly / Shutdown Turnarounds)
