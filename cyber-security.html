Industrial OT & SCADA Cybersecurity

Securing critical energy infrastructure, refinery control networks (DCS/PLC), and emergency shutdown systems against cyber-physical threats.

Operational Technology (OT) Security Standard

Protecting Plant Floor Control Networks (DCS, PLC, SCADA)

As oil refineries, chemical complexes, power plants, and manufacturing facilities digitize their operations, the line between IT networks and Operational Technology (OT) has blurred. A cyber intrusion on a refinery's Distributed Control System (DCS) or Emergency Shutdown (ESD) panel can cause catastrophic plant trips, physical equipment destruction, or environmental releases.

Omtek Corporation provides specialized Industrial Cybersecurity Advisory & OT Vulnerability Audits aligned strictly with IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82 standards, bridging process safety engineering with cyber defense.

Industrial Advisory Pillars

Our Industrial Cybersecurity Offerings

🛡️ IEC 62443 Audits

Zone & Conduit Segmentation

Designing robust network zones, conduits, and air-gapped firewalls between corporate IT networks and critical plant-floor OT control loops.

⚡ Cyber-HAZOP / PHA

Cyber Process Hazard Analysis

Integrating cyber threat scenarios into traditional HAZOP/HAZID process safety reviews to identify vulnerable sensors, PLCs, and safety instrumented functions (SIF).

📟 ESD & F&G Panel Defense

Safety Instrumented System Security

Securing SIL-rated safety instrumented systems (Triconex, HIMA, Yokogawa ProSafe) against unauthorized firmware tampering or network manipulation.

🔒 Vendor & OEM Access Control

Secure Remote Access Management

📋 OT Incident Response

Plant Cyber Incident Recovery

OT Cyber Safety Training
