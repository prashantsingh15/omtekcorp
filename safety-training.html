FIELD DISCIPLINE

Bridging Safety Protocols with Plant Realities

Industrial accidents rarely occur due to a lack of safety rulebooks—they occur because of complacency, inadequate hazard perception, ambiguous handovers, and unauthorized shortcuts under production pressure.

Drawing upon 39+ years of industrial plant experience across energy, chemical, and utility assets, Omtech Corporation delivers safety training grounded in physical plant realities. We train engineers, technicians, and contractor supervisors how to anticipate energy hazards, implement strict Lockout-Tagout isolations, execute dynamic Job Safety Analyses, and enforce Stop-Work Authority with technical authority.

Practical Isolation Drills Lockout/Tagout hardware simulation and zero-energy verification.

Contractor Safety Auditing and enforcing safety discipline on third-party labour teams.

SAFETY CATALOGUE

8 Core Industrial Safety Training Programmes

Comprehensive safety modules tailored to plant operating scopes, hazards, and participant levels:

ST-01 / OTC-20 | PERMIT & ISOLATION ⏱ 1 TO 2 DAYS

Permit-to-Work (PTW) & Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) Systems

Target Audience: Shift engineers, maintenance technicians, safety officers, and contractors involved in hazardous mechanical, electrical, or chemical interventions.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Hot Work, Cold Work, Confined Space & Height work permit protocols
• Energy isolation: Electrical breaker locks, valve blinds, and pneumatic bleeds
• Zero Energy State verification testing prior to breaking containment
• Shift changeover isolation handovers and group lockout boxes
Practical Drill: Physical lockout pad station simulation, group lockout box exercises, and permit audit flaw identification.
Syllabus 📄
ST-02 | HAZARD ANALYSIS ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Job Safety Analysis (JSA) & Hazard Identification

Target Audience: Frontline supervisors, maintenance engineers, and plant safety stewards responsible for daily task authorizations.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Step-by-step job breakdown for non-routine maintenance activities
• Identifying hidden energy hazards (hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, toxic)
• Applying the Hierarchy of Controls (Elimination → Substitution → Engineering → PPE)
• Conducting engaging, actionable Pre-Job Tool-Box Talks (TBT)
Practical Drill: Drafting real JSAs for live plant tasks (e.g. pump seal replacement, high-pressure line blinding) followed by critical peer review.
Details
ST-03 / OTC-19 | SAFETY CULTURE ⏱ 1 TO 2 DAYS

Behaviour-Based Safety (BBS) & Culture Transformation

Target Audience: Plant managers, EHS committee members, shift supervisors, and union/workforce representatives.

Key Learning Outcomes: • The ABC Behaviour Model (Activators → Behaviors → Consequences)
• Conducting peer-to-peer non-punitive safety observations
• Overcoming cultural barriers to near-miss reporting
• Positive reinforcement strategies that eliminate risky habits
Practical Drill: Live field observation walkthrough, coaching an unsafe behaviour role-play, and designing a plant BBS scorecard.
Syllabus 📄
ST-04 | CRITICAL LIFTS ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Lifting, Rigging & Crane Safety Awareness

Target Audience: Riggers, crane signalers, maintenance fitters, and construction/turnaround supervisors handling heavy machinery.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Sling angle tension calculations and Working Load Limit (WLL) charts
• Rigging hardware inspection (shackles, wire ropes, webbing slings rejection criteria)
• Standard hand and radio signaling protocols for mobile and overhead cranes
• Critical Lift Plan formulation (tandem lifts, lifts over live hydrocarbon pipes)
Practical Drill: Physical inspection of defective rigging hardware samples, center-of-gravity estimation exercise, and lift plan review.
Details
ST-05 / OTC-21 | FIRE PREPAREDNESS ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Industrial Fire Safety, Detection & First Response

Target Audience: Plant emergency response teams, facility engineers, warehouse managers, and security personnel.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Industrial fire chemistry, flashpoint dynamics, and flammable limits (LEL/UEL)
• Portable fire extinguisher selection and PASS technique execution
• Fixed suppression interlocks: Deluge skids, CO2, FM-200 hold time protocols
• Evacuation marshaling and liaison with municipal / industrial fire brigades
Practical Drill: Live fire extinguisher simulator or extinguisher discharge drill (subject to site arrangements) and mock plant evacuation timing.
Syllabus 📄
ST-06 | CONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT ⏱ 1 DAY / 8 HRS

Contractor Safety Governance & Turnaround Induction

Target Audience: Turnaround managers, procurement buyers, plant contract administrators, and resident contractor supervisors.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Pre-qualification safety audits and contractor capability scoring
• Site safety induction systems for migrant and temporary workforces
• Monitoring contractor daily compliance and penalty/reward mechanisms
• High-density turnaround safety governance and co-activity management
Practical Drill: Drafting an enforceable contractor EHS charter and conducting a simulated contractor safety violation inquiry.
Details
ST-07 | INVESTIGATION & RCA ⏱ 2 DAYS / 16 HRS

Incident Investigation, Evidence Gathering & RCFA

Target Audience: Safety managers, maintenance heads, lead investigators, and plant inquiry committee members.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Securing the incident scene and physical/digital evidence preservation
• Witness interviewing techniques that avoid intimidation and defensiveness
• Applying 5-Why, Fishbone, and TapRoot/Fault Tree models to safety events
• Developing Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) with SMARTheadlines
Practical Drill: Full investigation mock simulation: participants review witness statements, CCTV logs, and broken components to formulate the root cause report.
Details
ST-08 | CRISIS MANAGEMENT ⏱ 1 TO 2 DAYS

Emergency Preparedness, On-Site Plans & Crisis Command

Target Audience: Incident Controllers, Emergency Coordinators, senior plant management, and site security chiefs.

Key Learning Outcomes: • Designing and auditing On-Site Emergency Plans (OEP) per statutory norms
• Incident Command System (ICS) roles and decision hierarchy
• Toxic gas release modeling (plume dispersion) and wind direction response
• Media and community crisis communication protocols
Practical Drill: Table-top crisis command simulation with injected escalating events (power failure, toxic cloud shift, missing worker).
