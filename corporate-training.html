Masterclass Engineering Curriculum

Bridging Classroom Theory with Refinery Field Experience

Led personally by Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra (Ex - Kuwait Oil Company), our corporate training workshops move far beyond generic slides. Every module is anchored in real plant failure logs, P&ID schematics, and actual maintenance case studies from petrochemical refineries, fertilizer complexes, power plants, and heavy manufacturing.

We empower plant managers, maintenance engineers, safety officers, and quality auditors to systematically identify root causes, eliminate recurring equipment breakdowns, and maintain high MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures).

Masterclass Curriculum Modules

  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA), 5-Why & Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagrams
  • Six Sigma Green & Black Belt DMAIC Frameworks
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC) & Process Capability (Cp/Cpk)
  • FMEA, CAPA, Poka-Yoke & Zero-Defect Manufacturing
  • Reliability-Centred Maintenance (RCM) & PM Optimization
  • Asset Integrity Management Systems (AIMS)
  • Plant Shutdown & Major Turnaround Execution
  • Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) & 5S Implementation
