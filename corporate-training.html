Bridging Engineering Principles with Shop-Floor Execution

Led personally by Mr. Prem Prakash Mishra, drawing on 39 years of founder career experience across NTPC, IPCL, and Kuwait Oil Company (KOC), our training programmes are designed for real-world impact. Every module combines solid theoretical foundations with actual plant engineering case studies, P&ID schematics, and hands-on maintenance exercises.

We focus on practical plant capability — training engineers and technical managers to systematically analyze equipment behavior, improve mean time between failures (MTBF), reduce mean time to repair (MTTR), and sustain robust safety and quality standards on the shop floor.

Core Training Methodology Highlights

  • Real Plant Case Studies & Equipment Failure Logs
  • P&ID Schematic & Mechanical Blueprint Analysis
  • Interactive 5-Why, Fishbone & RCFA Group Drills
  • Customized Pre-Training Plant Needs Assessment

Priority Programme 01 3 - 5 Days On-Site

Centrifugal Pumps & Mechanical Seal Reliability

Target Audience: Mechanical Engineers, Maintenance Managers, Reliability Engineers & Plant Technicians.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • Pump hydraulics, BEP operation & NPSH margin calculation
  • Mechanical seal API 682 flush plans & failure mode identification
  • Vibration analysis, shaft alignment & bearing lubrication best practices
Practical Drills: Mechanical seal disassembly, flush plan troubleshooting & NPSH failure case studies.
Assessment & Takeaways: Post-workshop evaluation & plant seal life improvement checklist.
Priority Programme 02 3 - 5 Days On-Site

Compressors & Rotating Equipment Engineering

Target Audience: Operations Leads, Maintenance Supervisors & Machinery Engineers.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • Centrifugal & reciprocating compressor performance curves & surge control
  • Lube oil system maintenance, dry gas seals & coupling alignment
  • Condition monitoring, thermography & acoustic analysis
Practical Drills: Compressor surge control simulation & lube oil contamination review.
Assessment & Takeaways: Written assessment & machine health diagnostic template.
Priority Programme 03 2 - 3 Days Workshop

Root Cause Analysis (RCA) & 5-Why Problem Solving

Target Audience: Plant Managers, Quality Leads, Safety Officers & Maintenance Teams.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • 5-Why methodology, Ishikawa Fishbone diagrams & Apollo RCA
  • Evidence collection, physical vs. human vs. systemic root causes
  • Developing robust Corrective Action Plans (CAPA) with verification
Practical Drills: Live group RCA exercise using plant breakdown case logs.
Assessment & Takeaways: Plant RCA Facilitator Certificate & RCA reporting toolkit.
Priority Programme 04 3 Days Workshop

Reliability-Centred Maintenance (RCM II / JA1011)

Target Audience: Asset Managers, Reliability Engineers & Maintenance Planners.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • 7 RCM Questions according to SAE JA1011 standard
  • FMEA / FMECA development for critical plant systems
  • Optimizing Preventive Maintenance (PM) tasks to eliminate non-value PMs
Practical Drills: FMEA decision logic tree exercise for boiler feed pumps.
Assessment & Takeaways: RCM Practitioner certificate & PM optimization matrix.
Priority Programme 05 3 Days Workshop

Equipment Lifecycle Engineering & Maintenance Strategy

Target Audience: Plant Engineers, Shutdown Coordinators & Maintenance Planners.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) & lifecycle asset evaluation
  • Turnaround / Shutdown planning, S-curves & critical path management
  • Spares management, bill of materials (BOM) & vendor qualification
Practical Drills: Turnaround schedule simulation & spare parts criticality coding.
Assessment & Takeaways: Plant lifecycle management audit template.
Priority Programme 06 3 - 5 Days Certification

Quality Improvement, Six Sigma & CAPA / Poka-Yoke

Target Audience: QA/QC Managers, Process Engineers & Production Heads.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • Six Sigma DMAIC roadmap & Statistical Process Control (SPC / Cpk)
  • Mistake-proofing (Poka-Yoke) design & zero-defect manufacturing
  • Closed-loop Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) implementation
Practical Drills: Process capability calculation & Poka-Yoke fixture design drill.
Assessment & Takeaways: Quality Engineering certificate & SPC Excel templates.
Priority Programme 07 3 - 4 Days Workshop

HSE & Process Safety Management (PSM) Compliance

Target Audience: HSE Managers, Process Safety Engineers & Plant Operations Officers.

Module Outline & Learning Objectives:

  • OSHA 1910.119 14 elements of Process Safety Management (PSM)
  • Permit to Work (PTW), Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) & Job Safety Analysis (JSA)
  • Behaviour-Based Safety (BBS) observation & safety leadership
Practical Drills: JSA generation exercise & PTW audit roleplay.
Assessment & Takeaways: PSM compliance checklist & auditor certificate.
Priority Programme 08 2 - 3 Days Internal Auditor

ISO Management Systems & Internal Auditing

  • Internal audit planning, clause-by-clause evaluation & non-conformity (NC) writing
  • Management Review Meeting (MRM) preparation & audit reporting
Practical Drills: Mock audit roleplay & NC writing workshop according to ISO 19011.
Assessment & Takeaways: Certified Internal Auditor qualification & audit checklists.
